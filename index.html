How it works

1. Add your squads

Set how many squads are training and how many players are in each. Rename them to your club or team names.

2. Set the clock

Game length, rest time, an optional buffer for walking between pitches, and how long the whole session runs. The app works out how many rounds fit.

3. Choose the rules

Team size, whether squads play themselves or mix, and whether team-mates rotate. Balance uneven numbers splits everyone across the pitches so nobody sits out when the count is odd.

4. Run the session

Every player is tracked for games played and rest rounds, so you can see at a glance who is short on game time.
